Questions clients ask

The things people usually want to know before we start. Anything else, ask Helm or book a short call.

What time zone do you work in?

I’m based in Pakistan (UTC+5) and set my working hours around yours. Before we start, we agree a fixed daily overlap with your team, so questions get answered while your day is still running.

How do you handle access to our tools and data?

You stay in control. I work through my own user in your accounts with only the permissions the job needs, keep shared logins in a password manager and am happy to sign an NDA. When we finish, you remove my access in minutes.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No long lock-in. Work runs through Upwork, so you can start with a small fixed project or a few hours a week and scale up once it’s working. Contracts can be paused or ended at any time.

What happens in the first week?
  1. Day 1 Kick-off call, access set up, priorities agreed.
  2. Days 2–3 I learn how things run today and map the gaps.
  3. Days 4–5 The first fixes go live: triage rules, templates, a clean board.
  4. End of week A short written report: what’s done and what’s next.
How does handover work?

Everything I build is documented as I go: SOPs, templates and checklists stored in your tools, not mine. If you bring the work in-house or hire someone new, they get a written playbook and a recorded walkthrough.

What does the free audit include?

A 30-minute call where you show me your inbox, support queue or project board. I point out where time is leaking and send three fixes you can use straight away. No obligation and no sales pitch.
