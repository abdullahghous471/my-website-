# Helmwork outreach strategy (LOCKED, approved by Abdullah 2026-10-24)

Follow this exactly in every session. Change it only when Abdullah asks.

## Rules that never change
- **Nothing is sent without Abdullah's explicit "yes, send"** for the exact recipient and text.
- Send from helmwork.tech@gmail.com only after warm-up is finished and an inbox test scores well.
- Every email ends with the Helmwork signature (`outreach/signature.html`).
- Stop the sequence immediately when a prospect replies, says no, or the email bounces. A "no" is final.
- Business emails from public company websites only. Every email has an easy opt-out.
- Keep tokens low: work in batches, short replies.

## Targets
- Market 1: Spain, high-end agencies (luxury villas, new builds): Costa Blanca, Costa del Sol, Mallorca, Ibiza. Later: UK, UAE, Netherlands, Germany, US, Canada, Australia.
- A good prospect: sells premium homes, active, good reputation, website is a standard template with **no 3D/animation**, and missing things we offer (AI assistant, smart forms, online booking, multilingual).

## Research rules
- Check every point on the live website before using it, and recheck right before sending.
- Pitch business value, never trivial issues (no footer years, no image alt text, no generic "slow on phone").
- Never claim something we could not verify.

## What we pitch (mix per agency, never the same list for everyone)
3D animated website (Three.js) · AI assistant / AI concierge (24/7, multilingual, knows the listings) · smart enquiry forms that qualify buyers · online viewing booking · AI property matching and routing to the right agent · seller valuation request ("What is my home worth?") · multilingual site.

References in every first email: **likova.space** (3D style inspiration, never claimed as ours, never said it isn't ours) and **helmwork.pc-spain.workers.dev** (our own 3D site).

## The sequence (same thread)
| # | Day | Purpose | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email 1 | 1 | Pitch | One angle built on that agency's own story + the offers that fit them + 2 references. Unique structure and ending per agency. |
| Follow-up 1 | 3–4 | Nudge | One extra idea for their site, 2–3 lines. |
| Follow-up 2 | 8–9 | Offer | "Should I redesign your homepage as a free concept? If you like it, we jump on a call." Build the concept **only** for those who say yes. |
| Follow-up 3 | 15–16 | Close + other services | Polite goodbye on the website idea, then offer Helmwork's other services: virtual assistance, chat and customer support, operations and CRM (and other services). This part may be the same for everyone. |

## Volume
Warm-up first (about 14 days, hand-sent). Then 3–5 prospect emails a day, rising to 10 a day if replies are healthy and nothing lands in spam.
