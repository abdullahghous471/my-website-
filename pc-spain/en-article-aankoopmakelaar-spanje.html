Buyer’s agent in Spain

Buying a house in Spain. It sounds like a dream, but we make it reality. With our team of independent consultants and experienced agents we support you through the buying process. We offer expertise in several fields, so we do more than just the purchase: besides purchase guidance we also provide legal advice. That guarantees you a transparent process with clear agreements. This is how we make buying a house in Spain accessible to you – after all, the purchase procedure there is very different from the Netherlands.

Hiring a buyer’s agent in Spain

Buying a house involves all kinds of things, which leaves plenty of room for mistakes – not what you want during your purchase, especially when buying a home abroad. The procedure in Spain is also quite different: whereas in the Netherlands the seller pays the selling agent and the buyer pays the buying agent, in Spain the sale price includes a percentage that is shared between the selling and buying agent (except in the Valencia region).

As a buyer, it is important to know what you actually pay your buyer’s agent and what they do for it. Property Consultancy Spain is transparent about this. Based on your wishes we make a selection of suitable homes and point out the amenities in different neighbourhoods or in the region where you are looking. Our consultants will inform you about the costs in advance.

Unfortunately Spain is something of a jungle of estate agents, including some without experience. Our team, however, has been working in European real estate for more than 20 years, and our consultants often hold legal or real-estate qualifications. At PC-Spain you can count on the support of an expert who represents your interests throughout the purchase. And because we speak Dutch, English and Spanish, we can bridge the language and cultural differences.

You can also hire a buyer’s agent once you have already found your ideal home in Spain. The agent then advises you on the price and helps you with the next steps. By attending the viewing, we uncover the strengths and weaknesses of the house for you, and use them to help you negotiate the price. Our consultants also help you get the paperwork in order.

The advantages of a Dutch agent in Spain

Hiring a buyer’s agent is a good choice, especially in a housing market that is unfamiliar to you, like Spain’s. So what exactly are the advantages of a Dutch property consultant in Spain?

First, the Dutch buyer’s agent knows the Spanish housing market well – probably a lot better than you do. That makes it easy for the consultant to estimate the value of a home, and their experience in negotiating means you will end up paying a fair and good price.

Another advantage is that an agent is not emotionally involved in the purchase. Of course they know your wishes, but they can keep looking at the home rationally and point out any drawbacks, to prevent problems in the future.

A final advantage is that a Dutch buyer’s agent in Spain also looks at the legal side. Spain has its own laws and regulations around buying a house, and a property consultant will inform you about them. They also arrange the paperwork and make sure your documents are ready for the purchase.
