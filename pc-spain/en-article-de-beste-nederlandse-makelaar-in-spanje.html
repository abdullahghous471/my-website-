Dutch estate agent in Spain

Looking for the best Dutch property consultant in Spain? Makes sense. Buying a house is a big investment and you want expert advice – especially in a foreign country, with a foreign language and a different way of doing things (think of the siesta and an appointment culture of ‘mañana, mañana’). As a Dutch agent in Spain we know both cultures well, as well as the laws and regulations for buying a home. With us as your partner you can go through the process in your own language; your property consultant translates everything into Spanish language and customs.

With our knowledge and experience we advise you on the purchase and support you in making an offer. We guide you through the entire buying process, are always available for questions and go that extra mile for you. Want to learn more about the best property consultant in Spain? Read on or get in touch.

Buying a house in Spain

Buying a house is an important step in your life. That is true in the Netherlands, and even more so when you want to buy abroad. It brings risks and uncertainty, mainly because you are often not familiar with the laws of that country. An agent can help: they take you by the hand and guide you through the whole process. So want to buy a home abroad? Hire a property consultant. It saves you time, effort and a bad purchase.

Have you set your heart on a house in Spain – the warm, welcoming country where the kitchen always smells good? You will find a wide choice of houses and apartments. Whether you are looking for an authentic Spanish villa or an apartment in the centre of, say, Valencia, as a Dutch agent in Spain we are your partner in buying your home.

Your Spanish home through a Dutch consultant in Spain

Spain is a huge country, more than 12 times the size of the Netherlands. The culture is different, and so is the housing market – and there are big differences between its 17 regions, including in the homes themselves. So what should you look for in your search for your ideal Spanish home? A property consultant helps you set out realistic wishes that fit your budget. By asking the right questions, Property Consultancy Spain finds out what you are looking for in a home.

We search for property in the region you prefer, matching your wishes and budget. As the best agent in Spain we make the search for your ideal home a great success. Whether your Spanish home should be on the beach, right in the centre or in a picturesque village, we will find it for you.
