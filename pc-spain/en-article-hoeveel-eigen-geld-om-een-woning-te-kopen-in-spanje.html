How much of your own money do you need?

Do you want to buy a home in Spain, to live in or as an investment, but don’t know how much of your own money you need to put in? Don’t worry – we are happy to tell you. Applying for a mortgage in Spain works differently from the Netherlands. Our team is ready to answer all your legal and financing questions, and when you are ready to buy, you can contact us too. Our consultants specialise in several fields related to buying homes in Spain.

How much of my own money do I need?

You may have savings, liquid assets or equity in your home in the Netherlands, but there is a good chance you will need a mortgage to finance a home in Spain. For that you apply to a Spanish bank. A non-resident – so you, as a Dutch citizen – can receive a private mortgage of up to 70%. Through a company (BV) you can get a 50% mortgage. The Spanish bank calculates the mortgage on the lower of the purchase price and the valuation. So if the valuation comes in below the purchase price, the bank bases the mortgage on the valuation, which can change the amount of your own money you need.

For your own funds, this means you must finance at least 30% of the amount yourself. That is different if you emigrate to Spain and are already registered as a resident: then you can borrow up to 80%, so you finance 20% yourself. Buyer’s costs are never included in the financing.

So you need a reasonable amount of your own capital to buy a home in Spain. That doesn’t make the step any less worthwhile. Spain is a beautiful country where the sun shines 320 days a year.
