Buying a home in Spain

Buying property in Spain

Are you ready to take the step – to leave the Netherlands behind, wholly or partly, and move to Spain? Whether you are emigrating or buying a second home to escape the harsh winters, buying a house in Spain is a special and exciting step. That is why we want your search to go smoothly. Our team of experienced buyer’s agents helps you, with expert purchase guidance as well as legal advice and support. That ensures a transparent buying process, so you can buy your ideal home, loft or studio in Spain.

An agent for your purchase in Spain

Buying a house involves all kinds of things – as you probably remember from your last purchase in the Netherlands. Abroad, however, things work differently, with other laws and rules. That is why it is wise to hire an agent.

An agent helps you on several fronts. They can support you at the start of your search for a home in Spain, drawing up a list of all your wishes for the house, including your preferred location, nearby amenities and the size of the plot. The agent then makes a selection of available homes and sends it to you.

You can also choose to hire an agent later, for example once you have found a home yourself. The agent then helps you with the next steps, which mainly means doing everything possible to get you that home: advising you on the price and helping you negotiate. An agent also helps you get all the paperwork in order – they know what you need to buy a house in Spain!

Buying your ideal home in Spain

Whether you want to buy a house in Valencia or on the Costa Blanca, buying your ideal home in Spain doesn’t have to be complicated. Our experienced team of buyer’s agents is happy to help. We know the Spanish housing market well and are up to date on all the laws around buying a house in Spain, so we can advise you well and take the right steps, so the buying process doesn’t stall or fall through. Our knowledge also lets us judge the value of a home, which gives us an advantage when negotiating the price.

So there are several advantages to hiring a buyer’s agent. As a Dutch buyer’s agent, Property Consultancy Spain is a good partner during your purchase. Curious how exactly we can help? Get in touch and we will bring you closer to your ideal Spanish home!
