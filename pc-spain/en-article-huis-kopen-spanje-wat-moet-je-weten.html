Buying a home in Spain: what should you know?

Buying a house is not something you do lightly, especially abroad. Buying property in Spain is an exciting step that deserves good preparation. What should you know about buying a home in Spain? To help you on your way, we are happy to share what the buying process looks like. We have years of experience as a Dutch buyer’s agent in Spain, because we believe everyone deserves a smooth purchase with as few problems and uncertainties as possible.

Finding your home in Spain

The procedure for buying a home in Spain starts with the search, and it is important to know exactly what you want – you can’t just pop round to view a house. How many rooms do you want, which location do you prefer and which amenities do you want nearby? Still unsure? A buyer’s agent can help you get clear on what you are looking for. Together with you they draw up a wish list and select homes based on it, saving you a lot of time and effort. An agent knows the Spanish housing market like no other and finds the hidden gems for you.

Have your eye on a home? Then it is time to view it. An agent books the viewing trip for you and offers help and advice along the way, so you can be sure of making the right choice.

How do you buy a house in Spain?

Was the viewing a success and are you excited about the house? Then it is time to negotiate – and here too a buyer’s agent is a partner you want on your side. An agent has a lot of experience in negotiating the purchase price and can often get you a good deal. But they help with more than negotiating: they also inform you about all the costs of buying a Spanish home. You need your own funds, of course, but you also pay taxes and recurring costs. The agent arranges the documents you need and provides legal guidance, which makes buying a house in Spain a lot easier.

Buying a Spanish home through an agent

We have listed several reasons why it is wise to hire an agent when buying your house in Spain: the process becomes much simpler for you. We take care of everything during the purchase, because our team specialises in several fields, offering both purchase guidance and legal guidance. That keeps the process transparent. We make clear agreements and make sure you find your dream home in Spain. Want more information or ready to take the step? Get in touch!
