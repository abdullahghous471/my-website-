Help with buying a home in Spain

Buying a home in Spain is an exciting step, and for Dutch-speaking buyers the process isn’t always clear. The laws and regulations are different, another language is spoken and Spanish culture differs from Dutch culture. So it is very helpful to get expert help in that foreign country in your own language, from reliable, qualified and experienced consultants who know the procedures and can answer all your questions, for example about the mortgage. Would you like help finding and buying a home or investment in Spain? We will help you through the buying process, with both purchase guidance and legal advice, which makes it much easier for you.

The procedure for buying a home in Spain

To prepare you for what lies ahead, here is the procedure. Would you like our help with buying your home in Spain? We start with an introductory meeting. We want all your questions answered and to learn about your wishes. Because every home has different features and every client different wishes, our advice is tailored. Then we search for the ideal home for you in Spain. Once we have found it, we schedule a viewing – and of course we provide help and advice on your viewing trip and go with you.

Meanwhile our legal department is already at work. They check all the details, such as registrations and (occupancy) permits, and look into the background of the landlord/seller. You will also need to supply documents for this; we will let you know in good time so you can send them.

Once everything is in order and you are still excited about the home, we make an offer. We handle the negotiations on the purchase price – in consultation with you, of course, with clear agreements. Has your offer been accepted? Then we also finalise matters such as the financing with the Spanish bank. Finally, it is time for a toast – because we celebrate success together!

Applying for a mortgage for a home in Spain

If you need a mortgage to buy your home in Spain, we help with that too, through our Dutch partner Spaanse Hypotheek. They have several banks in their network and offer independent mortgage advice, so you know whether you need a mortgage or not. If so, they help you apply. We make not only the purchase but also the mortgage application simpler – which makes us your partner for buying property in Spain!
