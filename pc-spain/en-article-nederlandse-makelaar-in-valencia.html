Dutch estate agent in Valencia

A house in a country where the sun shines 320 days a year and it is almost always warm – who wouldn’t want that? As a Dutch agent in Spain we help you find a home in the beautiful city of Valencia, where you can choose a location in the city centre, on the beach or along the lovely Turia, for example. Whether you are looking for a second home for the winter or emigrating to Valencia, you don’t want to run into too many obstacles during the search and purchase. We make sure the process runs smoothly. Our consultants know the city inside out and are happy to help you buy property in Valencia. They know the do’s and don’ts and know the different neighbourhoods like the back of their hand. Benefit from our service – we are always there for you, so that you can soon enjoy your Spanish home!

Buying a home in Valencia

Valencia is the third-largest city in Spain: a beautiful, vibrant city on the Balearic Sea (part of the Mediterranean). Valencia has everything you need in daily life, so it is no surprise you want to buy a home here! It has a lively old town with many museums and churches. The beautiful centre full of shops can keep you busy all day, and there is also a large city park and of course a promenade for lovely walks.

Whether you are moving to escape the cold winters or perhaps to start a business in Spain, Valencia is a great choice. You will want for nothing here.
