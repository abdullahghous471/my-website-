Procedure for buying a home in Spain

We guide you in buying your ideal home in Spain. We do this because we are passionate about our work and in love with the country where you are looking for a home. We are committed to making your property purchase a worry-free period – whether you are looking for a (second) home for yourself or want to make a smart investment.

The search for a home

When you join Property Consultancy Spain, we schedule a call or an appointment in person in the Netherlands or Spain. In this conversation we explain how things work and discuss your wishes regarding location, size, layout and year of construction. We share our own experience, but above all we listen to which features matter to you. Together we see whether we can lay a good foundation for working together. We then send you the start-up invoice.

Once we have agreed what you want to buy, we make several homes available through our partners (after that invoice has been paid). We plan two to three days in Spain to drive around with you in person and view homes. During these viewings you can expect plenty of advice from us, to help you make the best choice.

If you are looking for property as an investment, we only show you properties that are suitable for it. If you are looking for a second home, we also explore the area together to see whether the region suits you.

The purchase procedure for your Spanish home

Once you have found your ideal Spanish home during the viewings, we make sure it becomes yours. Before an offer is made, you are first informed about all purchase costs, other recurring costs and taxes, so you can decide properly whether you can and want to make an offer. Of course, as your buyer’s agent we are there for you in this decision. Together we decide on a strategy so we make the right offer. Our team then starts the negotiations, aiming for the best price and/or terms.

During this process we also help you apply for an NIE number, the Spanish tax number you need before the purchase takes place. We help you open a Spanish bank account and put you in touch with a lawyer – and, in the case of an investment, a tax adviser or gestor. Our consultants help you draw up a reservation contract and review all the property documents to get a clear picture of the condition of the home and the permits. When everything is in order, we book a date with the notary.

Unfortunately no suitable home among them? Then we will do our best over the following months to find one after all, either with you on location or by video call if you are not in Spain yourself.

A provisional purchase contract

Depending on the seller’s terms, there may first be a provisional purchase contract (Contrato de Arras Penitenciales). You usually pay a 10% deposit of the purchase price after 45 days. This is paid to the seller, a contract is drawn up, and the date of the transfer is agreed.

We hope the procedure for buying a home in Spain is now a bit clearer. If not, you can always contact us. We are happy to explain everything down to the last detail, so you are assured of a pleasant and clear buying process.
