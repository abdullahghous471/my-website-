Purchase commission in Spain explained

Scouring the Spanish housing market for your ideal home is an exciting time – one that asks a lot of you, in both energy and effort. You are often unfamiliar with Spanish laws and regulations, and the whole buying process can be overwhelming. That is why many people choose to hire a buyer’s agent, who helps you in both the orientation phase and the purchase, making the process a lot easier. But an agent doesn’t work for nothing. Here we explain the purchase commission in Spain – the amount an agent charges for their services.

Why does a buyer’s agent charge commission?

First it helps to understand what a buyer’s agent actually does. Buying a home in Spain involves a lot. An agent is your partner throughout the process and takes care of everything. They scour the Spanish housing market for you and find gems that match your wishes. Once they have found your ideal house, they move on to the negotiation and achieve a cost saving for you. For their time and effort, they charge a commission.

Agents’ commission in Spain

In Spain the commission for the buying and selling agents is usually included in the purchase price, so the visible price includes commission. As a Dutch buyer’s agent in Spain, we share this commission with you. We believe our cooperation should be transparent, so we are not secretive about our rates. We share the difference between the purchase price before and after our mediation: when we save on the initial asking price – our profit – we share it with the client. That gives you a discount on the purchase price.

Buying a home in Spain through Property Consultancy Spain

Transparent amounts and sharp negotiations. We are committed to getting a fair, market-based price for buyer and seller, using our knowledge and experience to achieve it. By negotiating we realise a saving on the purchase price for you – and, just as with the commission, we share that saving.
