Apartment Altea sea view

This high-quality apartment is right on the beach. It has 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms and underfloor heating throughout. The living room has a natural-stone feature wall, the kitchen is open-plan and the apartment is furnished with solid wood furniture. Electric awnings in the living room and master bedroom keep it cool, and the home also has air conditioning and built-in appliances, and comes furnished.

This lovely home, with a 25 m2 terrace, has wonderful views of the Mediterranean and the garden with its large swimming pool. It is part of a new, gated apartment complex on the beach. Good to know: the apartment is wheelchair accessible and only a ten-minute walk from Altea town centre.

The price includes an underground parking space.
