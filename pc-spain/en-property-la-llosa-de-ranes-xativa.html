Ref: La Llosa de Ranes – Xàtiva – TR

Three-bedroom finca near Xàtiva

If you love peace and quiet and beautiful green surroundings – and would like to earn something too – this modern 3-bedroom finca of 100 m2 on around 30,000 m2 of land full of citrus trees could be just right. The citrus harvest yields between 18 and 20 thousand euros a year.

There is water and more than enough green power – solar panels and a wind turbine – to run the whole house. The house has a swimming pool with a spacious terrace and breathtaking views.

La Llosa de Ranes is 3 kilometres from one of the most beautiful inland towns, Xàtiva, which has an English international school if you have children of school age. There is plenty of culture and lovely little restaurants, so you won’t be lonely here.

The price is excellent and earned back within 10 years!
