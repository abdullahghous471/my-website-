Luxury apartment Valencia

The apartment is on the 18th floor and has 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and a 27 m2 terrace with spectacular views.

Located in one of the most luxurious residential complexes between the Costa de Valencia and the Costa de Azahar, next to one of the best beaches of the Mediterranean. This beautiful complex offers two types of homes: standard and premium.

A parking space and storage room are included. On the 20th floor of the complex there is an infinity pool with panoramic views, as well as a gym, sauna and padel courts. The complex has 24-hour security.

The apartment is just a few minutes’ walk from the beach, a 25-minute drive from the city of Valencia and 30 minutes from Valencia airport.
