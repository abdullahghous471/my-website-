Beautiful villa Moraira

This beautiful modern villa has wonderful sea views and is in a quiet neighbourhood. The beach and the centre of Moraira are both only 2.5 kilometres away.

The home is spread over 3 floors. The ground floor has 1 bedroom with en-suite bathroom, a toilet and the kitchen, plus the generous living room opening onto the 48 m2 outdoor terrace and the large pool.

The first floor has 3 bedrooms, each with its own bathroom, and two terraces. The basement has a large 44 m2 garage, and outside there is a carport and room to park several vehicles.

The whole home has underfloor heating, air conditioning, electric shutters and an indoor alarm. It also has solar panels and several fitted wardrobes, as well as a 22 m2 barbecue area, laundry room, landscaped garden with automatic irrigation and automatic gates.

This house faces south.
