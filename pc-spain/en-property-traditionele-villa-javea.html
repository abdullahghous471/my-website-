A traditional villa, ideally located for amenities including a supermarket, restaurants and an international school, and just 450 metres from the lovely cove of Cala Blanca. Arenal beach is a 20-minute walk.

This beautiful traditional villa was designed by a renowned architect and has many of his signature features. It has 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, an open living/dining room with fireplace, kitchen, covered terrace and roof terrace, all surrounded by a level garden with lawn, planted beds and palm trees, with room for a private pool. There is a garage and off-road parking, plus a bus stop nearby, and open green views towards the Montgó.

The ground floor has 2 spacious bedrooms with fitted wardrobes and en-suite bathrooms; both bedrooms open onto the main terrace. There is also a guest toilet, a modern, fully fitted kitchen with built-in top-brand appliances and a separate utility room with laundry that opens onto its own terrace. The open kitchen connects to a large living and dining room with beautiful views through large windows onto the spacious sun terrace with a 66 m2 infinity pool and a barbecue. Upstairs there are 2 more bedrooms, both with fitted wardrobes, en-suite bathrooms and private terraces.

The whole property is surrounded by a beautiful, fully landscaped garden with automatic irrigation. The plot has 2 entrances with automatic gates, each with parking for 2 cars. Extras: barbecue area, infinity pool, indoor pool, sauna, gym, wine cellar, garage for 3 cars, parking for 4 cars, 2 entrances, landscaped garden with automatic irrigation, ducted air conditioning, underfloor heating, home automation, indoor/outdoor alarm system + pre-installation for cameras, lift, south facing, sea views, automatic entrance gates.
