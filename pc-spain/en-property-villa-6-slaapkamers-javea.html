Villa with 6 bedrooms in Jávea

A villa on a 1,941 m2 plot with 248 m² built, divided into three parts: the main house on one floor, a self-contained two-bedroom apartment on the ground floor and a self-contained studio on the top floor.

Entering through the electric gates you find gravel parking for many cars and a separate lockable pedestrian gate. The mature, well-kept gardens offer lots of room to make your mark, and there is even space to build further if needed. There is a barbecue area, an outdoor shower and plenty of covered areas to enjoy outdoor living out of the glare of the sun. The pool is larger than average at 12 m x 6 m and is surrounded by a Spanish tosca-stone patio. The whole large plot is securely fenced and completely private. More than half of the plot borders a ‘Zona Verde’, the equivalent of Green Belt land in the UK.

The main villa has three bedrooms with fitted wardrobes, 2 en-suite bathrooms and a further separate bathroom, together with the living room, kitchen and separate utility room.

The self-contained apartment has a living/dining room and kitchen – a lounge with two sofas, a fully equipped kitchen including washing machine and a dining table for six – 1 bedroom with fitted wardrobe, storage and an en-suite with shower, vanity unit and toilet, and 1 further bedroom with fitted wardrobe; next to bedroom 2 is a separate toilet and a separate shower and basin.

Another self-contained apartment, reached by an outside staircase, is a studio: the living and sleeping area has a large wardrobe and a TV/relaxation area. The kitchen has everything you need to be self-sufficient, and there is a separate large shower room with basin, a separate toilet and a washing machine. The studio has the benefit of its own private roof terrace.

The property is conveniently located just a few minutes’ drive from several supermarkets and other amenities, while Arenal beach is just over 5 minutes away by car.
