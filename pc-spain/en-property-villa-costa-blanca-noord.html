Villa on the Costa Blanca North

This traditional 4-bedroom villa is on a private 1,500 m² plot close to Arenal beach and its amenities. It is reached by a private driveway and has views of the open sea and the valley.

The grounds are largely low-maintenance, with typical Mediterranean trees and plants and some lawns.

The lower floor has a generous garage next to a large storeroom, with good potential for further development if needed, and a bedroom suite with a large bathroom.

The first floor has 3 more double bedrooms and two bathrooms, together with the large living room leading to both the kitchen and the spacious naya (covered terrace) – the place to enjoy the beautiful views. The property is offered in good condition and also has good potential for further development.
