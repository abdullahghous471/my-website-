Working method

Working method – Buying a home in Spain

You can engage us to buy a home in Spain. You engage a team of personal advisers, and as standard you receive purchase guidance and legal guidance. Need additional advice? Then you can expand the team. Below you can see how buying a home works.

01

Getting to know you

We listen to the client’s wishes and needs. What are the preferences? A villa in Moraira or an apartment in a city like Valencia? Every type of home has its own characteristics, so the client already receives tailored advice here. The client’s financial possibilities are also reviewed in this phase.

02

House hunting

Together we search for your Spanish home or investment! You can expect an active attitude from our advisers. Is your ideal home not among our listings? No problem, we look further with fellow agents – anything to find a home that matches your wishes!

03

Planning a viewing

A home that matches your wishes has been found! We value personal attention throughout the process, so you also receive help and advice when booking a viewing trip.

04

Ideal home or investment found

In Spain it is very important to buy a home without defects. That is why our legal department already checks the property in this phase. Are the registrations and (occupancy) permits correct? Is the background of the landlord/seller in order?

05

Price agreement

A worry-free home starts with making good agreements. We handle the negotiations on the purchase price or the monthly rent, and we stand on the client’s side – after all, our commission was communicated at the start. No hidden deals, but transparent agreements!

06

Deal!

After a price agreement, certain matters are wrapped up – for a purchase, think of the financing with the Spanish bank. After that, we can raise a glass together!
