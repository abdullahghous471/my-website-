Plain-English glossary

Beneficiary

The person or entity you name to receive the death benefit. A primary beneficiary is first in line; a contingent beneficiary receives it if the primary cannot.

Death benefit

The tax-free lump sum paid to your beneficiary when you die. Also called the face amount: the core reason most families buy life insurance.

Living benefits

Riders that let you use part of your death benefit while alive after a terminal, chronic or critical illness diagnosis.

Rate class

The health and lifestyle category an underwriter assigns you (Preferred Plus, Preferred, Standard or Substandard). It directly sets your premium.

Underwriting

How an insurer reviews your age, health, lifestyle and family history to decide whether to offer cover, and at what rate.
