      Term or whole life: which do I need?

      Term covers a set number of years and costs the least. Whole life lasts your whole life and builds cash value, so it costs more. Many families mix both. Joseph shows you real quotes for each before you decide.

      How much coverage does my family need?

      A good start is the DIME method: Debt, plus Income for the years your family depends on you, plus Mortgage, plus Education, minus what you already have. We calculate it together in your review.

      Is the protection review really free?

      Yes. It costs nothing and there is no obligation. If you do not need more coverage, Joseph will tell you.

      Why use an independent agent?

      A captive agent can only sell one company's policies. An independent agent compares many carriers, so the recommendation follows your needs, not a sales quota.

      Can you review a policy I already have?

      Yes. Bring any policy you own. We read it together, explain what it really covers, and show you if anything is missing or overpriced.

      Which states do you serve?

      Joseph is licensed in Illinois (No. 1356850) and in multiple other states. Carrier and product availability varies by state and eligibility.
