Add it to Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Open Gmail → ⚙ Settings → See all settings.
  3. In the General tab, scroll to Signature → Create new → name it "Helmwork".
  4. Click into the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose "Helmwork" for new emails and replies.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
